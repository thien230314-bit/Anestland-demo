# Floating contact and pre-footer assets

All runtime assets are stored locally; no image hotlinks or icon dependencies.

## App shortcut vectors

- `icons/contact-messenger.svg`: Messenger mark geometry by Facebook, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Facebook_Messenger_logo_2025.svg). Adapted to a white mark on a circular blue app canvas; source geometry retained.
- `icons/contact-zalo.svg`: Zalo app artwork by VNG Corporation, sourced from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Icon_of_Zalo.svg), whose original source is zalo.me. Only circular canvas clipping was added; lettering is vector paths, not substitute font text.
- Commons marks both simple logos PD-textlogo, with trademark restrictions still applicable. These assets identify existing demo contact shortcuts and do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
- `icons/contact-phone.svg`: Bootstrap Icons `telephone-fill` geometry, with a local burgundy circular background and two call-wave accents. [Original source](https://github.com/twbs/icons/blob/main/icons/telephone-fill.svg), [MIT license](https://github.com/twbs/icons/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Decorative transition

- `images/decor-swiftlet.png`: built-in Imagegen warm-gold natural-history-style bird cutout with alpha transparency; reused with a mirrored transform for the right bird.
- `images/pre-footer-nest.png`: generated using the built-in Imagegen tool with genuine alpha transparency. Illustrative edible-nest still life, not a photograph of a commercially available ANestLand product. No previous product image was replaced.

### Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent decorative still-life cutout for ANestLand premium Vietnamese edible bird's nest website, displayed on deep warm burgundy. Subject: a small elegant low oval dark walnut wooden tray with three beautiful ivory edible swiftlet nests arranged naturally, one standing upright showing delicate fine strands and two gently resting in front. Only tray and edible nests, no birds, no plants, no bowls or other props. Premium realistic studio product photography, warm cream ivory fibers, rich dark wood, soft warm light from above left and subtle rim light, detailed natural fine nest texture. Composition: isolated centered wide horizontal arrangement viewed at a gentle eye-level three-quarter angle; complete entire tray and all nests visible, compact shape filling most of the wide canvas, modest transparent margin. Background genuinely transparent alpha with clean natural edges, no rectangular backdrop or floor, no strong cast shadow; website will supply its own soft shadow. No text, no watermark, no packaging, no gold ornaments. Visually refined and believable, not an illustration.

### Bird generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one decorative bird cutout for a luxury Vietnamese bird's-nest website, to be displayed small on a deep burgundy background. Primary request: a single anatomically elegant swiftlet in flight, facing right, long slender crescent-shaped swept wings spread gracefully in a natural three-quarter flying pose, small streamlined body, short subtly forked tail. Refined natural-history engraving with delicate, realistic feather detail and softly shaded warm antique gold and muted champagne ivory tones, restrained premium editorial style. Complete bird visible, isolated with genuinely transparent alpha background and clean edges. Composition: horizontal bird, most of the canvas occupied by the subject, no extra scene or objects. It must look like a graceful real swiftlet, not an eagle, pigeon, cartoon, angular logo or crude clip art. No text, no frame, no emblem, no watermark, no glow. Only one bird. Avoid heavy black outlines, bright saturated yellow, plastic shine, blue or green hues.
