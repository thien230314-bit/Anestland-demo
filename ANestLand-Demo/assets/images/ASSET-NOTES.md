# ANestLand demo imagery

All seven images were created with the built-in image generation tool for this educational demo. They are illustrative product mockups, not photographs of commercially available ANestLand products. No Oars images or artwork were copied.

## Generation prompt set

Create six photorealistic product and editorial photographs in a 3-column, 2-row contact sheet. Use premium Vietnamese edible bird's nest, pale sage green and ivory cream, realistic texture, soft natural lighting, and no text or watermarks:

1. A tilted glass jar of translucent ivory bird's nest dessert with red dates, a matte sage lid, and a blank cream label on an ivory background.
2. An upright glass jar of bird's nest dessert with lotus seeds, a sage lid, and a blank cream label on an ivory background.
3. A shallow porcelain bowl holding ivory dried edible swiftlet nests with intricate thread texture.
4. A sage gift box containing three small dessert jars, with its lid beside it.
5. An overhead editorial still life of bird's nest dessert with red dates, a wooden spoon, linen, and a fresh green sprig.
6. Hands carefully cleaning an ivory edible bird's nest using fine tweezers beside linen and a bamboo tray.

The sheet was cropped into six JPEG assets and resized to 800 × 800 for use in the site.

## Hero cutout prompt

Extract the single glass jar from `red-date-jar.jpg`. Remove the ivory background and floor. Preserve the glass jar, sage lid, blank cream label, ivory nest strands, red dates, original tilted pose, and photographic texture. Output a photorealistic isolated jar on a genuinely transparent background with a subtle contact shadow. No added decorations.

Saved as `hero-jar.png`. Floating leaves, dates, seeds, and organic shapes are separate CSS decorations.
